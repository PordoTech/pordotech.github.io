Pordo brings music, video and comics together in one app: the Windows and macOS
desktop clients offer the full feature set, and the Android app covers music,
comics and cloud sync. You can optionally connect AI services (subtitles,
lyrics, comic translation) and your own WebDAV storage.

## Where to start

1. [Install & Update](/en/guides/install-update/): pick the package for your platform, and learn how updates and macOS signing work.
2. [Getting Started](/en/guides/getting-started/): open your first file, basic controls and platform differences.
3. Then read the feature guides below as needed.

You can also go straight to the [download page](/en/download/) for the current release.
