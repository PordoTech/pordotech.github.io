## Search and bookshelf

1. Open the comics page and search the **MangaYun** or **Komiic** sources.
2. The two sources stay separate: matching titles are not merged and results are
   shown per source.
3. Open a title, choose a chapter and start reading; add books to your bookshelf
   to check for updates and resume from history.
4. Komiic chapter lists can be filtered by chapter/volume type.

A new favorite starts with the existing chapter list as its baseline; later
unread additions produce update indicators.

## Reading modes

- Three modes: vertical scrolling, left-to-right pages, right-to-left pages.
- Zoom, page position and reading progress are saved automatically.
- The chapter button opens the chapter list; the page slider and previous/next
  controls navigate within the book.
- Keyboard Left / Right turn pages in the reader.
- On Android, a chapter is marked read only after scrolling to the bottom of the
  last decoded page; opening a chapter by itself never clears unread updates.

## Komiic account (optional)

Signing in provides account-level image quota and persistent sessions; without
an account the app stays in guest mode.

| Client | Entry point |
| --- | --- |
| Desktop | Settings → Comics → Komiic account |
| Android | Comics page → “Source settings” |

1. Enter the registered email and password, enable “auto sign-in”, then choose
   “Save and sign in”.
2. Expected result: nickname, sign-in status and the image quota returned by the
   official site are displayed.
3. With auto sign-in disabled you can still sign in manually for the session;
   after a restart the app returns to guest mode.
4. “Sign out and clear password” keeps the email, disables auto sign-in and
   deletes the local password — even if the remote sign-out request fails, the
   local session is gone.

<Aside type="note">
Credential storage: Windows Credential Manager, macOS system Keychain, and
KeyStore-protected encrypted storage on Android. Passwords are never written to
plaintext config or logs. The Komiic account, password and session cookies are
**not** part of WebDAV / API-key sync.
</Aside>

## Cache

- Original pages and covers are cached locally; cached pages stay readable when
  a source is unavailable or its image quota is exhausted.
- Clearing caches in Settings preserves translations, favorites and history.
- Some upstream sources need adapter updates or are unsupported; for persistent
  failures check or file
  [Issues](https://github.com/PordoTech/pordo/issues).

## Comic translation (desktop only)

After adding a compatible service under **Settings → AI Providers** and choosing
an **image-editing** model in the comic settings, you can translate a page or a
whole chapter, with original, translated and side-by-side views. Translation is
an explicit action and may incur service charges. Full steps and limits:
[AI guide](/en/guides/ai/#comic-translation).

The Android app currently has no comic-translation UI.

## Cloud sync

Comic favorites (including tracked titles you unfavorited), chapter lists,
read/update markers and reading progress sync between desktop and Android via
WebDAV; see [WebDAV Sync](/en/guides/webdav-sync/).

## Common failures

| Symptom | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Source search fails or images won't load | Upstream outage, exhausted quota or an adapter update may be needed; cached pages stay readable |
| Komiic sign-in fails | Verify email/password and account activation; after repeated failures the app pauses auto-retries — fix the credentials and retry manually |
| Progress missing on another device | Confirm both ends use the same WebDAV account with sync enabled; see the sync guide's conflict and offline handling |
